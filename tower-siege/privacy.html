Tower Siege – Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to the Tower Siege game for Android (package com.maxdflamegaming.towersiege) and the Tower Siege web game, made by maxdflamegaming.

The short version

Tower Siege does not sell or share your personal information. It has no ads, no third-party analytics and no in-app purchases. The campaign plays offline. For online play (PvP, the leaderboard and clans) the game has a small account of its own, with no email and no real name. To find levels that are too hard, the game sends anonymous level stats (level numbers, times and results), which you can switch off in Settings.

What the game keeps on your phone

A small file in the game's own private storage holds your progress (levels, stars, coins, upgrades and looks), your settings, the name you picked, your online account's id and secret key, and level stats waiting to be sent. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Online play: PvP, the leaderboard and clans

When you open PvP, the leaderboard or clans, your phone connects to the Tower Siege game server (hosted by Render, render.com, with its data in a Postgres database) and sends your account id and secret key, the name you picked and, during a match, your moves. The server keeps an account for you with: that name, your trophies, wins and losses, your clan, when the account was made and last used, and reports other players made about your name. Other players see your name, trophies and clan. There is no chat.

As with any internet connection, the server sees your phone's IP address while you're connected. It uses it only to limit how many new accounts one address can make in an hour, keeps it in memory for at most an hour, and never stores it. Accounts that never finished a match are deleted after 30 days without a visit.

Names that are rude are refused, and a name reported by several players is replaced.

Anonymous level stats

When you start a campaign level, the game notes the level number; when it ends, whether you won (and in how many seconds, with how many stars), lost (and why), or left it. These notes are sent to the same server in batches, at most every half hour. They carry no account, no device id and no name, and the server only keeps totals per level (for example, "level 12: started 500 times, won 61%"), to find levels that are too hard. Switch Share anonymous level stats off in Settings and the game stops noting them and forgets any it hadn't sent.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes everything on your phone. To have your online account deleted from the server, open an issue (link below) with the name you play under and roughly when you last played online, and it will be removed. Level stats can't be deleted individually because they aren't linked to anyone.

Permissions

The game asks for two permissions: internet, for online play and the level stats, and vibration, to buzz your phone on captures and losses (you can turn this off in Settings). It does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

Children

The game doesn't ask for anyone's age, email or real name, and there is no chat. Online, players see only the names others chose. A child can play the whole campaign without going online.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting more (for example, if ads are added), this policy will be updated first, and the change will be described in the game's store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to delete your account? Open an issue at github.com/maxdflamegaming-byte/New-game/issues.

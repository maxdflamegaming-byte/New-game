# Color Claim – Privacy Policy

*Effective 30 September 2026 · Applies to the Color Claim game for Android (package com.maxdflamegaming.colorclaimhd), made by maxdflamegaming.*

## The short version

Color Claim does not sell or share your personal information, and it has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. Playing offline, nothing leaves your phone. Playing **Online**, the game sends the server only what it needs to run the game you're in (below), and the server keeps none of it after you leave. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your save is also kept in your own Google account (below).

## What the game keeps on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file in its own private storage on your device. It holds:

- your coins, level, XP, stats, trophies, missions and daily streak;
- the skins, trails and pets you've bought, and which ones you're using;
- your settings (sound, music, vibration, controls and graphics);
- the name you type on the Profile screen, if you change it (it is only shown in your own game).

This file is never sent to us. It is not included in Android backups, and uninstalling the game deletes it. The only copy that can leave your phone is the cloud save in your own Google account, and only if you sign in with Google Play Games (below).

## Online play

When you choose **Online**, your phone connects to the Color Claim game server (hosted by Render, render.com) and sends:

- the name you chose on the Profile screen (or "Player"), your colour, and the skin, trail and pet you're using, so other players in your room can see them;
- your steering while you play.

The server sends every player in the room the names, colours, looks and positions of everyone in it. As with any internet connection, the server sees your phone's IP address while you're connected; the game doesn't record it. Names are checked for rude words. When you leave the room, the server forgets you: nothing about you or your games is stored on it. Your coins, XP and progress stay on your phone as always.

## Google Play Games (optional)

Versions of the game that include Google Play Games sign-in show it under **Settings → Google Play Games**. Signing in is optional; the game works the same without it. If you sign in:

- Google shares your Play Games player ID and gamer name with the game. The game uses the name only as your Online name if you never chose one.
- **Cloud save:** a copy of the save file described above goes to your own Google account through Google Play Games, so your progress can come back on a new phone. It is stored by Google, not by us, and we can't read it. You can delete it in the Google Play Games app or in your Google Account settings.
- **Leaderboards:** your best claim and number of wins are sent to Google Play Games leaderboards, which other players can see alongside your gamer name.

Google's handling of this data is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Games Services terms](https://developers.google.com/games/services/terms).

## Permissions

The game asks for two permissions: **internet**, used for Online play and, if you sign in, Google Play Games, and **vibration**, to buzz your phone when you're knocked out or claim land (you can turn this off in Settings). It does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

## Children

The game doesn't ask for anyone's age, email or real name. Online, players see only the in-game names others chose; there is no chat. If you're a parent and want to keep your child offline, choose any mode other than Online.

## Changes

If the game ever starts collecting more (for example, if ads are added), this policy will be updated first, and the change will be described in the game's store listing.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Tower Siege is a quick, colourful strategy game: drag a road from your tower to another, and your soldiers march along it. Bring a tower to zero and it's yours. Every tower you hold trains more soldiers, so the map turns your colour one building at a time.

60 STORY LEVELS
Chase General Grumble's Red Army across 12 chapters and 4 lands: Green Valley, Sunny Desert, Frosty Peaks and Palm Beach.
• The first 5 levels teach you step by step, with a hand that shows each move.
• 11 boss battles, each made by hand with its own twist: a castle behind walls with one gate, a moat, a line of bunkers, an empty castle you can take and turn on Grumble...
• Missions change from level to level: take everything, defend your headquarters, capture the radars, win with no reinforcements, or beat the clock.
• Duke Mustard's Yellow army and Major Moss's Green army join the fight, and fight each other too.

THEN ENDLESS MODE
After level 60, endless levels keep coming, each a little harder than the last.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Tank factories, bunkers, watchtowers, training camps and walls change every battle.
• Airstrikes and rallies, building upgrades, and upgrades you keep between battles.
• Stuck? Tap 💡 Hint and the game shows you a good move. While you drag, a label tells you if you have enough soldiers.
• Daily quests with chests, a daily reward and a daily challenge map.
• Hats for your soldiers and flags for your towers.

PLAY OTHERS
• Online 1-vs-1, private rooms with a code, a leaderboard and clans.
• Two players on one phone, or practice against a bot.

Works offline (online play needs a connection). In English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Indonesian, Russian and Turkish.
```

## Notes

- Don't advertise a bigger level count than 60. The endless levels after 60 reuse the same
  level maker with stronger enemies; they're a mode, not new content.
- Contains no ads and no purchases yet (they're switched off in the build); update the
  Play Console "Contains ads" and "In-app purchases" answers if that changes.
